How to play

Paint the elements. See what they do.

Everything follows simple physics. Heavy things sink, liquids spread out, heat flows from hot to cold, and materials melt, boil, freeze and burn at their real temperatures. Mix things and see what happens.

There is one world. 3D shows all of it; 2D is a cut through it, like looking at the side of an ant farm. Move the cut with the Slice slider or W S. Whatever you do in one view is there in the other.

2D side view

  • Left mouse paint
  • Right mouse erase
  • Scroll or pinch to zoom · Middle-drag pan
  • Shift + Scroll brush size
  • W S move the slice
  • T thermal camera

3D voxel world

  • Left mouse paint
  • Right-drag turn around the island (top, sides, underneath)
  • Scroll zoom · Middle-drag pan
  • A D orbit · two fingers on touch

Everywhere

  • 1–0 elements · E H J tools
  • [ ] brush size · + − zoom
  • Space pause · . step
  • M switch 2D/3D · R reset · C clear

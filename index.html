How to play

Paint the elements. See what they do.

Everything follows simple physics. Heavy things sink, liquids spread out, heat flows from hot to cold, and materials melt, boil, freeze and burn at their real temperatures. Mix things and see what happens.

Mouse

  • Left mouse paint
  • Right mouse erase
  • Scroll zoom toward the mouse
  • Middle-drag move around
  • Shift + Scroll brush size

Touch

  • One finger paints
  • Two fingers pinch to zoom and drag to move around

Keys

  • 1–0 elements · E H J G B L W N F M tools
  • [ ] brush size · + − zoom · arrows move
  • Space pause · . step · T thermal camera
  • R reset · C clear

Creatures: pick one under Creatures and click to drop it in. Click and drag a creature to pick it up and throw it. They walk about, run from fire, swim, drown, burn, and get hurt by falls, acid, lava and heavy loads. The Blood button turns blood and gore on or off.

New: drop someone onto Rubber, pop some Corn, pile up Uranium, or let a Black hole loose. Draw walls with the Line tool.

Try this first: pour lava into the pond, drop a seed on the grass, or set the tree on fire with the Heat tool. Then light some thermite on metal, lay a fuse to a pile of gunpowder, or switch the weather to Storm.
